The Three Little Pigs

Once upon a time, three little pigs built three houses. The first house was made of straw, the second of sticks, and the third of bricks. The big bad wolf blew down the straw house and the stick house. The frightened pigs ran into the brick house and locked the door. The wolf stood outside and shouted, "Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house down!"
